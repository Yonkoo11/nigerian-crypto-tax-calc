Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator accurate?

Yes. The calculator uses Nigeria's official 2026 progressive tax brackets (0% to 25%) from the Nigeria Tax Act, 2025, verified against EY, PWC, and KPMG analyses. However, this is an educational tool - for your specific situation, please consult a qualified tax professional.

Is my data safe? Do you store my information?

100% private. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We don't have a backend server, database, or analytics tracking your income data. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

What is the small player exemption?

To qualify for the small player exemption (pay ₦0 tax), you must meet BOTH conditions in any 12-month period:

  • ✅ Gains under ₦10 million (your profit = sale price - purchase cost)
  • ✅ Proceeds under ₦150 million (total amount you received from selling)

Example: If you sold ₦200M of crypto but only made ₦5M profit, you do NOT qualify (proceeds too high). Both thresholds must be met. Consult a tax professional to confirm your eligibility.

What if I made over ₦50M?

At high income levels (₦50M+), professional tax planning becomes crucial. Consider consulting a crypto-savvy tax advisor who can help with strategies like corporate structures, business expenses, and other legal optimization methods.

Do I still need an accountant?

This calculator helps you understand your tax liability, but you'll still need professional help for actual tax filing, especially if you have complex transactions, multiple income sources, or want personalized tax planning advice.

What are the 2026 tax brackets?

Nigeria's 2026 crypto tax uses progressive brackets (official rates):

  • • ₦0 - ₦800k: 0% (tax-free)
  • • ₦800k - ₦3M: 15%
  • • ₦3M - ₦12M: 18%
  • • ₦12M - ₦25M: 21%
  • • ₦25M - ₦50M: 23%
  • • Above ₦50M: 25%

Source: Taiwo Oyedele, Presidential Fiscal Policy and Tax Reforms Committee
