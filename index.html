Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Nigerian crypto taxes in 2026

What crypto income types are taxable in Nigeria?
All crypto income is taxable: capital gains from trading, mining income, staking rewards, airdrops, yield farming, freelance payments in crypto, NFT sales, and business revenue from crypto activities. The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 explicitly covers digital assets.
How is the Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) exemption calculated?
SMEs with capital gains below ₦10M AND annual turnover under ₦150M are fully exempt from capital gains tax. If you made ₦8M from crypto trading and your total business turnover was ₦120M, you owe ₦0 in capital gains tax.
What deductions can I claim to reduce my tax?
You can claim: pension contributions (8% or 10%), housing allowance, health insurance premiums, life insurance, 20% rent relief (if you're a tenant), and National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) contributions. These deductions can save you ₦400,000+ annually.
Is this calculator 100% private and secure?
Yes. All calculations happen in your browser using JavaScript. We don't send your data to any servers, we don't store it, and we don't track it. No accounts required. You can even use it offline after the page loads. Your financial privacy is guaranteed.

Yes. Our calculations are based on the official Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and cross-verified against tax analyses from PWC Nigeria, EY, KPMG, and the Presidential Tax Committee. The progressive tax rates (0%, 15%, 18%, 21%, 23%, 25%) are accurate as of January 2026.
