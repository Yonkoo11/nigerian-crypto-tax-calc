📋 Accordions (FAQ Style)

What crypto income types are taxable in Nigeria?
All crypto income is taxable including capital gains, mining income, staking rewards, airdrops, and business revenue from crypto activities.
How are capital gains calculated?
Capital gains = Selling price - Purchase price (original cost basis). This applies when you sell, trade, or spend cryptocurrency.
What is the SME exemption?
Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) with capital gains below ₦10M and annual turnover under ₦150M are exempt from capital gains tax.
