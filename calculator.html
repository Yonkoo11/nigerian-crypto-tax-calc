Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator accurate?
This calculator uses official tax brackets from the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. However, tax laws can be complex. For personalized advice, consult a licensed tax professional.
What is the small player exemption?
Small traders pay ₦0 tax if BOTH conditions are met in a 12-month period: (1) Gains under ₦10M, AND (2) Total proceeds under ₦150M. Both thresholds must be satisfied.
What deductions can I claim?
Common deductions include pension contributions (100%, no limit), NHF/housing (100%), health insurance (100%), and 20% of annual rent paid (max ₦500k). All reduce your taxable income.
Do I need to report airdrops?
Yes. Airdrops are taxable income in Nigeria. The fair market value at the time you receive them is considered income. Keep records of dates and values for tax filing.
What if I made over ₦50M?
Income above ₦50M is taxed at 25% (only the amount above ₦50M). At this income level, professional tax planning can save significant amounts through legal strategies. Consult a tax advisor.
When do I pay my taxes?
Tax filing deadlines vary. Generally, individuals file annually. Check with the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) for specific deadlines and payment methods. Keep detailed transaction records year-round.
